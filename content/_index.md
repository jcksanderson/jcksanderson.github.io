Hi! My name is Jack and I'm a fourth-year undergrad at the University of Chicago studying statistics and computer science, as well as working part-time for [Gray Swan](https://www.grayswan.ai/) and [Second Look](https://secondlookresearch.com/). I'm largely interested in AI safety and interpretability, with the goal of making AI systems more transparent and surfacing their failures. 

In the past I've been a visiting researcher at EPFL, looked into [iterative LLM finetuning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.01130), investigated [sequential data poisoning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.04929), and created an [AI Security Guide](https://xlabaisecurity.com/) with [Zephy Roe](https://zephaniahdev.com/). Further in the past I investigated [compute-algorithm interactions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.04075), which was featured on Epoch AI's [Gradient Updates](https://epoch.ai/gradient-updates/how-fast-can-algorithms-advance-capabilities).

Read some of my random current thoughts [here]({{< ref "thoughts/index.md" >}}), and catch me at [UChicago AI Safety](https://uchicagoaisafety.com/) events!
